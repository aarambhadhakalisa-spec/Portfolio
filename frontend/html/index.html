Mount Everest

Mount Everest is the highest mountain on Earth, standing at 8,848.86 meters above sea level. It is located on the border between Nepal and China and is famous for its stunning scenery. Every year, many climbers visit Everest to experience the challenge of reaching its summit.

Chinese New Year

Chinese New Year is an important celebration in Chinese culture and is also known as the Spring Festival. It is a 15-day holiday where families come together, enjoy traditional food, exchange gifts, and decorate their homes. Fireworks, lanterns, and traditional dances are also popular parts of the celebration.
